How we help

Careful evaluation, attentive listening, a plan to get you thriving.

We'll help you build up your strength, improve your balance, and take on the world — or at least the stairs to your front door — with confidence.

Physical therapy

Getting you back on your feet, one small step at a time.

  • Strength and balance
  • Mobility after surgery, stroke or injury
  • Safe movement around your home

From dressing to driving, we help you turn everyday tasks into everyday victories.

Occupational therapy

Take back control of your daily routine and regain your independence and confidence.

  • Dressing, cooking and self-care
  • Daily routines adapted to your home
  • Confidence with everyday tasks

Whether you're struggling to find the right words, stuttering, or having trouble swallowing, we're here to help you regain your voice.

Speech therapy

Patient, compassionate support so you can communicate and safely enjoy meals with the people you love.

  • Finding words and communicating
  • Stuttering
  • Swallowing safely
