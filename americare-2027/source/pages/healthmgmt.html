How we help

Choose a condition to see what care looks like.

Stroke

Recovery, independence and safety after a stroke.

  • A personalized care plan aligned with your loved one's health needs and recovery goals
  • Rehabilitation support from physical, occupational and speech therapists to foster independence
  • Medication management, so the right medicines are taken at the right times
  • Emotional support, a listening ear and companionship
  • A home environment that's safe and comfortable

Parkinson's disease

Care that adapts as the disease progresses.

  • A care plan built around the disease's progression
  • Physical, occupational and speech therapy
  • Help following medication schedules, critical for managing symptoms
  • Companionship and emotional support
  • Home safety recommendations

Multiple sclerosis

Steady support for daily life with MS.

  • Monitoring of symptoms
  • Medications at the correct dosage and on schedule
  • Help with bathing, dressing, grooming and meal preparation
  • Physical therapy for strength, flexibility and balance
  • Emotional support and companionship
  • MS education and resources
  • Safety assessments and recommendations for a secure home

Heart disease

Heart-healthy routines, watched over at home.

  • Monitoring of blood pressure, heart rate and oxygen levels
  • Medication management
  • A personalized heart-healthy meal plan
  • Encouragement of physician-recommended activity
  • Lifestyle guidance on stress and quitting smoking
  • Help recognizing warning signs

Hypertension

Blood pressure, kept in check.

  • Regular monitoring of blood pressure readings
  • Medication management
  • Diet and exercise guidance
  • Education on hypertension awareness, self-care and changes to look out for

Diabetes

Confident daily diabetes management.

  • Personalized nutrition guidance for balanced, healthy meals
  • Regular health checks to monitor and manage blood sugar
  • Training on insulin administration and glucose monitoring
  • Emotional support for the challenges of living with diabetes

Palliative care

Comfort, dignity and quality of life.

We also address spiritual, emotional and psychological concerns for patients and families.

  • Relieving pain and achieving comfort
  • Enhancing quality of life
  • Compassionate support for patients and family members
  • Maintaining dignity
