Things you need to know

Home health aides (HHAs) do basic health tasks, such as checking vital signs and helping with prescribed exercises, under a nurse's supervision. Personal care aides (PCAs) give non-medical personal support.

Matching starts with an in-home assessment of your loved one's condition, routine and personality.

They help with activities of daily living such as bathing, grooming and dressing, and with household tasks.

Choose a PCA if the person is medically stable. Choose an HHA if they need medical monitoring, mobility help or help with exercises.
