Which kind of help?

Nurse, home health aide or personal care aide?

The right level of care depends on the person's health. Not sure? Our nurses do an in-home assessment and recommend a plan.

 Skilled nurse (CHHA)Home health aide (HHA)Personal care aide (PCA)
What they doHealth monitoring, medication management, wound care, patient and caregiver education, care coordinationPersonal care plus basic health tasks such as vital signs and prescribed exercisesNon-medical help with bathing, grooming, dressing and household tasks
Medical careYes, delivered by registered nursesBasic, under a nurse's supervisionNo
Best forComplex health needs, recovery after a hospital staySomeone who needs medical monitoring, mobility or exercise helpSomeone medically stable who needs help with daily living
